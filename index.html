Ski Crazy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026
App: Ski Crazy (Android package name: com.aphelionstudios.skicrazy)
Developer: Aphelion Games (“we”, “us”, “our”)

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Ski Crazy (the “App”). It explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and who it is shared with. In short: Ski Crazy does not collect your personal data, does not send any data off your device, and does not share data with anyone.

1. Information the App collects

The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. We do not run any servers that receive data from the App, and the App does not have permission to use the internet.

To work as a game, the App stores the following information only on your own device:

InformationWhy it is storedWhere it goes
The player name you type in (this can be any name or nickname you choose)To show your name on the results screen and the high score boardStays on your device only
Your chosen outfit colourTo display your skierStays on your device only
Your best scores, times and medals for each slopeTo show the high score board and your progressStays on your device only
Your Music and Sound effects on/off settingsTo remember your audio preferencesStays on your device only

This information is kept in the App’s private storage on your device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings, it is deleted. Please do not enter your real full name or any sensitive information as a player name.

2. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect or access: your location; your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone; your device’s advertising ID or other device identifiers; your email address, phone number or any account details; payment information; or any usage analytics or crash reports sent to us.

3. Advertising, analytics and third-party services

Ski Crazy contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking or software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. The App is built with the open-source Godot game engine, which does not send data to us or to third parties from the App.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share user data with any party. That includes advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, social networks, service providers and partners. We do not sell personal information.

Please note that the store you download the App from (for example, Google Play) operates independently and may process information about your purchase and device under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal information from the store about you beyond the aggregated sales and download figures it shows developers.

5. Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children specifically. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, including children, no special collection of children’s data takes place. Parents and guardians may wish to remind children not to enter their real full name as a player name.

6. Data security and retention

Because all information stays on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We keep no copies. Stored game data remains until you delete it by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App.

7. Your rights and choices

You are in control of all information the App stores. You can change your player name at any time from the slope selection screen (“Change Skier”), and you can remove all stored data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. If you are in the UK, European Economic Area or another region with data protection rights and you have any questions about them, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect. Any version of the App that begins collecting personal data would first require an updated policy.
